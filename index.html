01 — About

About us

Avanza is a 360-degree service provider in the logistics domain, with expertise in 3PL services, transportation services and manpower management. We are headquartered in Kochi, with a network across South India.

Avanza is the birthchild of a very ambitious group of professionals who believe in contributing to logistics growth in India, and in a position in this industry guided by the principles of Reliability, Trust and Adaptability.

ABS Group, pioneers in manpower services for the last few decades and an ISO 9001:2015 company, is proudly powering this venture.

Our management team brings a cumulative experience of over 100 years, which benefits every customer who uses our service. We are determined to consolidate our expertise in logistics and make our clients proud of our services for a very long time. Based in Kochi, we provide services in Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka.

3PL & CFA

Flexible, industry-ready solutions

We provide fully integrated services for warehousing, distribution and order fulfillment. Service is based on customer preference, and a client can choose from single PL to 3PL.

We provide VAS (value-added services) that include billing, documentation, collection accounting, customer service, and break-bulk and packing. We are fully statutory compliant, and we are committed to meeting every statutory requirement.

Clients can choose from a basket of services. Solutions can be custom-made to the organization’s requirement.

Platform

Intelligent trip tracking and delivery management

TRAEZ is our cloud-based logistics platform for real-time tracking, automated driver dispatch, trip booking and delivery monitoring. It is built for transparency, efficiency and speed — one place to manage every leg of delivery operations.

Features include document upload, delivery confirmation, driver ratings and fully customizable reporting. A modern interface and a capable backend keep logistics management straightforward.

It delivers end-to-end trip and delivery management with real-time tracking, automation and transparency, cutting costs and idle time while maximizing driver efficiency.
